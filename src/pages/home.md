I’m an industrial designer, educator, and researcher with a PhD in Industrial Design. My background brings together hands-on design practice, teaching, and research. Before moving into academia, I worked as an industrial designer in different sectors, and for the past eight years, I’ve been teaching design studio and visualization courses at TOBB ETÜ in Ankara. My work focuses on studio education, visualization, and the evolving role of technology in design. I was a Fulbright scholar at Carnegie Mellon University and previously studied at TU Delft as part of the Joint Master’s program in Design for Interaction.
